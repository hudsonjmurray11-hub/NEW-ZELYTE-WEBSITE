01 — Who we are

02 — What we collect

We process information you provide through the launch list, your site account, and Shopify checkout and customer accounts. Our service providers also process the technical information needed to operate these services.

The launch list

If you submit the launch-list form, we store your email address, your flavor preference if you select one, and which signup form you used.

Your site account

If you create an account, we store your email address, your name, your flavor preference if you set one, whether you opted in to marketing, and a hashed password we can never read. Your account is also issued a sequential early-access number and a single-use launch code.

Shopify checkout and customer account

When you use Shopify checkout, Shopify processes the contact, billing, delivery, order and subscription information needed to complete and manage your purchase. Payment details are handled by Shopify and its payment providers; this website does not receive your full card number.

Your site account and Shopify customer account are separate. Signing into this site does not sign you into Shopify. The pack builder sends Shopify your selected pack, flavor mix and purchase option; it does not automatically send your site-account email or user ID.

03 — Why we collect it

  • Your email, from the launch list — to send you one email when the first batch ships. That is the entire purpose, and it is the basis on which you gave it to us.
  • Your account details — to give you an account, keep you signed in, and send confirmation and password-recovery messages.
  • Order and subscription details — to process payment, fulfill and support your order, manage recurring deliveries, and send order, shipping and account messages.
  • Marketing opt-in — to know whether you want anything beyond the launch email. If you did not tick it, you do not get any.

04 — Who else sees it

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We have never done either.

These services support the website and its connected shopping experience:

  • Supabase hosts the launch-list and site-account database and handles site authentication and account-email requests. Your email, name, flavor preference and hashed password are stored there.
  • Vercel serves this site and its server endpoints and records standard server logs, including IP addresses.
  • Shopify provides checkout, payment processing, orders, subscriptions and the Shopify customer account, including related transactional messages.
  • Klaviyo, when our launch-email integration is enabled, processes launch-list profiles and preferences and manages opted-in marketing subscriptions.

We will also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone’s safety.

05 — How long we keep it

Launch-list emails are kept until the launch campaign is finished or you ask us to remove yours, whichever comes first. Account data is kept while your account exists and is deleted within 30 days of you closing it, except where we have to keep something to meet a legal obligation.

06 — Cookies and tracking

The custom website uses your browser’s localStorage to remember site sign-in and launch-popup preferences. The site session token authenticates requests to Supabase, and signing out removes the local sign-in session.

Shopify checkout and customer accounts use their own cookies and similar technologies for functions such as shopping sessions, authentication and security. Shopify may also use analytics technologies on those pages. See Shopify’s cookie policy for details.

07 — Your rights

Wherever you live, you can email hudsonjmurray11@gmail.com and ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it. We will not treat you differently for asking.

If you are in California

Under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA you have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing for that last one to switch off. You may use an authorised agent.

If you are in the UK or the EEA

Under the UK GDPR and GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Our lawful basis is your consent for marketing email, and the performance of a contract for your account. You may withdraw consent at any time and complain to your local supervisory authority.

Every marketing email carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing is always enough — you never have to email us to stop hearing from us.

08 — Children

This site and this product are not for anyone under 18. ZELYTE contains caffeine. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and we delete it if we discover we have.

09 — Changes

If we change this policy we will change the date at the top. If a change is significant — a new category of data, a new purpose, a checkout — we will say so on the site before it takes effect.

10 — Contact

Privacy questions and requests, and anything else: hudsonjmurray11@gmail.com. See also our terms of service and shipping and returns policy.
