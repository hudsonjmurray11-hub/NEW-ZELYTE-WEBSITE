Questions

The three we get most.

01What is a buccal pouch?

A small pouch that sits between your gum and upper lip. The tissue there absorbs directly, so the contents release steadily across the window instead of arriving all at once. Nothing is swallowed. More on how that works.

02How do I use one?

Between gum and upper lip, flat side against the gum. Leave it there for the 25-minute window, then take it out and bin it. Do not chew it and do not swallow it. Start with one.

03How much caffeine, and how many a day?

40mg per pouch. Use 1–3 per session and no more than 6 in 24 hours, which is 240 mg of caffeine. Check with a clinician first if you are sensitive to caffeine, pregnant or nursing, under 18, or taking medication.
