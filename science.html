The science

Absorbed where you put it.

A ZELYTE pouch sits between your gum and upper lip. Saliva dissolves the contents, and the tissue lining the mouth takes them up steadily across a 25-minute window — no bottle, no swallowing, no pause.

00:00Active window25:00

Buccal delivery

Place. Activate. Perform.

01

Place

The pouch goes between the gum and the upper lip. It stays put through movement, so it works while you do.

02

Activate

Saliva begins dissolving the blend within seconds. The mineral salts and caffeine go into solution against the gum line.

03

Perform

Release continues across roughly 25 minutes rather than arriving in one hit. Take the pouch out when the window closes.

ZELYTE supports hydration and provides caffeine. It is not a medicine and makes no claim to improve performance outcomes.

Magnesium malate was chosen over oxide because it dissolves readily at the pouch's moisture level and carries far less bitterness. Caffeine anhydrous is used because the dose is precise and stable in a dry pouch.

Use and safety

Read this part.

  • One at a time

    Use one pouch per 25-minute window. Take it out when the window closes.

  • Caffeine load

    Each pouch carries 50mg. Count it alongside coffee and anything else you drink that day.

  • Not for everyone

    Not recommended for children, or for those pregnant or nursing.

  • Check first

    If you are sensitive to caffeine or taking medication, speak to a clinician before using ZELYTE.
