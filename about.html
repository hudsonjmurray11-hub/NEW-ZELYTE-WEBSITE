Our story

Built by someone who kept stopping.

ZELYTE started with a specific, repeated problem: being deep into an effort, needing electrolytes, and having no way to take them without breaking the session.

00:00Active window25:00

Founder

A thousand meters in, with a dry mouth.

"I knew the feeling of being 1,000 meters into a race with a dry mouth and cramping legs — and knowing a bottle was not going to help me in that moment."

The idea came out of years of rowing, wrestling and lifting: sports where you cannot stop, cannot carry anything, and where the moment you need hydration most is the moment it is hardest to get. ZELYTE is the answer to that specific problem, not a general one.

Hudson Murray — Founder & CEO

Milestones

Where we are.

  1. Origin

    The idea takes shape during high school rowing, wrestling and lifting — sports where stopping to drink is not an option.

  2. Formula

    Four compounds settled: sodium chloride, potassium chloride, magnesium malate, and 50mg of caffeine anhydrous.

  3. Format

    The 25-minute active window is set, and the tin is designed around it — the timing rule is printed on the lid.

  4. Fall 2026

    First batch. Two flavors, Crispy Mint and Black Cherry, 15 pouches per tin.
