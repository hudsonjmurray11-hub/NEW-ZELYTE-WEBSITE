Frequently asked questions

The product

01What is ZELYTE?

An active hydration pouch. It sits between your gum and upper lip and releases electrolytes and 50mg of caffeine across a 25-minute window, so you can hydrate without stopping to drink.

02What is in it?

Four compounds: sodium chloride (115mg), potassium chloride (40mg), magnesium malate (25mg) and caffeine anhydrous (50mg). No sugar. The full table is on the science page.

03How many pouches come in a tin?

15 pouches per tin. A single tin is $11, the Pro Pack is 3 tins for $30, and the Elite Pack is 5 tins for $45.

04What flavors are there?

Two at launch: Crispy Mint and Black Cherry. Both carry the same formula — only the flavor changes.

Using it

05How long does one last?

The pouch is built around a 25-minute active window. Most of the contents have been released by the end of it. After that, take it out.

06What does it feel like?

A light tingle and a clear flavor at the start, easing off as the window runs down. The caffeine comes through gradually rather than in one hit, because it is released across the 25 minutes rather than swallowed at once.

07When should I use one?

Most people put one in a few minutes before the hard part of a session, or partway through a long one. It is designed for use while moving, so you do not need to stop or carry anything.

08How much caffeine is in it?

50mg of caffeine anhydrous per pouch — roughly half a cup of coffee. If you are sensitive to caffeine, pregnant, nursing, under 18, or taking medication, check with a clinician first.

09Can I use more than one?

Use one pouch per window. If you run several across a day, count the caffeine alongside coffee and anything else you drink.

10Do I swallow it?

No. The pouch stays between your gum and lip for the window, then comes out. Nothing needs to be swallowed for it to work.
