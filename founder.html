Founder

1,000 meters in.

ZELYTE started in a racing shell, in the part of a race where stopping is not one of the options.

The row

You cannot stop and you cannot carry anything.

I rowed through high school. In a boat you can't stop and you can't carry anything. 1,000 meters into a race my mouth was dry, my legs were cramping, and a bottle was no help. So I built something you can take without breaking stride.

Hudson Murray, Founder and CEO

The problem

Every option assumed you could stop.

A bottle needs a free hand and a moment to use it. Rowing has neither, and neither do most of the places the work actually gets hard.

01

Hands occupied

An oar, a bar, a set of handlebars, a patient. The moment a drink needs a hand, it needs the session to pause.

02

Volume you do not want

Sloshing half a liter is its own problem mid-effort. The electrolytes were never the heavy part — the water carrying them was.

03

All at once

A drink or a gel arrives in one hit and is gone. The back half of a session is long, and it wants something that lasts through it.

The build

Under the lip, for 25 minutes.

The answer was already in the mouth. Buccal tissue absorbs directly, so a pouch can release across a window instead of arriving in one swallow — and it needs no hand, no water, and no pause.

  1. Nov 2025

    Won Pitch George, the startup pitch competition at George Washington University. That was the first time the idea had to survive being explained to people who owed it nothing.

  2. Jan 2026

    Incorporated as ZELYTE Inc.

  3. Apr 2026

    Signed a Swedish pouch manufacturer to produce at scale. Sweden makes more oral pouches than anywhere on earth, and the tolerances that matter here — fill weight, seam strength, how long a pouch holds together under the lip — are ordinary work there.

  4. Aug 2026

    Samples in hand. Two flavors, one formula, 15 pouches a tin: 55mg sodium and 40mg caffeine per pouch, and nothing in the copy the formula table does not support.

  5. Oct 2026

    First production run.

What's next

We make one thing.

The first production run is October and the first batch is limited. The launch list orders before anyone else.

  • One product

    Two flavors of the same pouch. Nothing else gets built until this one is right.

  • No claims we cannot hold

    Potassium and magnesium are in the pouch and are deliberately never headlined — at 13mg and 12mg they would not survive the question, so we do not ask you to take them seriously.

  • Made in Sweden

    Produced by a manufacturer that has been making oral pouches far longer than we have.
